Medical imaging platform

MRI Annotation

A browser platform for annotating tumours in MRI scans: view a scan in 2D and 3D, draw the tumour slice by slice, and get a clean 3D volume ready for AI. Students are scored against an AI segmentation or a specialist’s reference. I built it from A to Z.
